EngChatt Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use EngChatt, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account data: such as your name, email, username, profile photo, and language preferences.
  • Usage data: how often and how long you use the app, lessons completed, AI interactions, and learning progress.
  • Device data: such as IP address (and derived location), device model, OS version, browser info (if applicable), and app crash reports.
  • Communication data: feedback or questions you send us, including support messages.
  • Payment data: if you make purchases via Apple or Google Play, your payment is processed by them. We do not access your full card or bank information.
  • Cookies and analytics: we use analytics tools and similar technologies to understand usage and improve our service.

EngChatt is designed for children aged 4–10 and is intended to be used under the supervision of a parent or guardian and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please email us at hi@meettheet.com.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver and improve the EngChatt learning experience
  • Personalize your content and progress tracking
  • Provide support and respond to inquiries
  • Analyze usage trends to enhance the app
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or legal violations
  • Comply with laws and regulations

We may also create anonymous or aggregated data that cannot be used to identify you for research and development.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers: such as hosting providers, analytics platforms, customer support tools, and payment processors.
  • Authorities and legal parties: if required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety.
  • Business transfers: such as in the case of a merger, sale, or restructuring of the business.

4. Where We Store Your Information

Your data may be processed and stored in the United States, Singapore, or other countries. If you are in Europe, this may involve transferring your data to countries outside the EEA or UK. We use standard contractual clauses and other safeguards where applicable.

5. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure. Be careful with what you share, and always keep your login credentials safe.

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Update or delete your account
  • Request a copy of your personal data
  • Ask us to correct, restrict, or delete your data
  • Object to how we process your data
  • Request data portability (if applicable)

To exercise your rights, email hi@meettheet.com.

7. Third-Party Links

EngChatt may contain links or integrations with third-party services (e.g., YouTube, support tools). We’re not responsible for how those third parties handle your data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you via the app or email. Continued use of EngChatt after an update means you accept the new policy.

9. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we have about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

We do not sell your personal information. To make a request, email hi@meettheet.com.

10. European Users (GDPR)

If you are in Europe, we act as the data controller of your personal data. You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct or delete your data
  • Object or restrict processing
  • Receive your data in portable format
  • File a complaint with your local data protection authority
